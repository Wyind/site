About

Hey, I'm wyind. I make stuff for Linux. Mostly browsers, TUI tools, and whatever else sounds fun at 2am.

My daily driver is a desktop running Ubuntu 26.04. The ThinkPad X13 Gen 2 is a separate setup and stays on Fedora. There's also a T420 sitting in a box with a broken screen — I'll fix it eventually, who really cares :)

I'm really into right to repair. Things should be built to last, be fixable, and not need a subscription to exist. Same reason I love Casio watches and ThinkPads: honest, durable, repairable hardware. I keep thinking about a Fairphone 6, but I haven't committed yet.

Outside of games and code, I like cat gifs quite a bit.
